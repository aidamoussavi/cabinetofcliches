The Format

One Cliché. A Cabinet of Curiosities.

1. The prompts

At the prompt drop, every entrant receives the same assigned cliché: perhaps a character archetype, a familiar trope, or a clichéd expression to work into their story.

They also receive seven curiosities, which may be objects, characters, settings, words, or atmospheres. Include at least one; you can use all seven.

2. The writing

Write a new story of up to 1,000 words in nine days. Each round spans at least two weekends.

3. The submission

Instructions and the submission link will be emailed to registered entrants. Submissions are judged anonymously.

4. The results

Every eligible story receives written feedback. Judges evaluate structure, prose, character, originality, and how effectively the prompts are used. A grand-prize winner and three runners-up are selected.
