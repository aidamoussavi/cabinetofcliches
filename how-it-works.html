FIELD NOTES / 02 — THE CHALLENGE

One cliché.
Seven curiosities.

Nine days to produce an original work of flash fiction. Here is how each round unfolds.

HOW IT WORKS01 / The prompts02 / The writing03 / Submissions04 / Results05 / Example round
01 — THE PROMPTS

The cabinet opens.

At the start of a round, all registered writers receive the same assigned cliché. It might be a character archetype, an overfamiliar plot device, or even a clichéd phrase to include in the story.

Alongside it comes a cabinet of seven curiosities. These can be objects, characters, settings, words, or an atmosphere. You must use at least one, but you are welcome to use all seven.

02 — THE WRITING

Nine days. 1,000 words.

Write an original story of up to 1,000 words. Each competition spans at least two weekends so that the challenge can fit around work, family, and everything else.

03 — SUBMISSION

Send it in.

Submission instructions will be emailed to entrants when the round begins. Stories are judged anonymously; do not include your name within the story itself.

04 — THE RESULTS

The reveal.

Every eligible entry receives written feedback. Judges assess structure, prose, character, originality, and creative use of the prompts. One grand-prize winner and three runners-up are selected.
