PROVISIONAL RULES — REGISTRATION NOT YET OPEN

These are a summary of the proposed rules, not finalized official terms. Legal and operational details must be resolved before registration and payment collection begin.

01 — PARTICIPATION

Eligibility & registration

The proposed competition is open to writers aged 18 or older. International participation is planned, subject to applicable laws and the availability of prize payments. One entry per person per round is permitted.

The proposed registration fee is $20 USD, with payments collected through Zeffy. Registration closes when the prompts are released. Exact times and dates will be announced in Eastern Time.

02 — THE WORK

Stories & prompts

Every round has one mandatory cliché and seven curiosities. Entrants must meaningfully incorporate the cliché and at least one curiosity into an original work of fiction of no more than 1,000 words, written specifically for that round.

Previously published stories, plagiarism, unauthorized fan fiction, entries exceeding the word limit, and late submissions are not permitted. Stories should not include identifying information because judging is blind.

03 — AUTHORSHIP

Human-written work

Submissions must be written by their entrant without generative-AI assistance in brainstorming, drafting, editing, or revision. Ordinary spell-check and voice-to-text tools are permitted. Final rules will define how suspected violations are reviewed.

Erotica, unlawful content, and material promoting hatred or discrimination are outside the contest's stated scope. Harassment of organizers, judges, or participants may result in removal from the competition.

04 — EVALUATION

Judging & feedback

Entries are judged anonymously on structure, prose, character, originality, and use of the prompts. Eligible submissions receive written feedback.

Stories that omit required prompts may receive feedback but are ineligible for prizes. Disqualified entries are not guaranteed feedback or refunds. Detailed procedures will be provided in the official rules.

05 — RIGHTS & AWARDS

Copyright, prizes & cancellations

Entrants retain copyright in their submissions. Any permission granted to the Ubergroup to publish a winning story or promote its award will be defined in the final contest terms.

The proposed prize pool includes a $150 overall winner and three $50 runners-up. International payment methods and jurisdiction-specific restrictions are still being determined. A cancelled round will result in refunds to registered participants.

06 — QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do I have to use all seven curiosities?

No. At least one curiosity must appear in the story; you can include more.

Do I get feedback if I don't win?

Yes, provided the entry is eligible under the finalized rules.

Can I enter internationally?

International entry is planned, but country eligibility and prize-payment methods have not been finalized.

Who owns my story?

You retain copyright. Any publication permission for selected stories will be described in the final terms.
