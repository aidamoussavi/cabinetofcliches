Rules & FAQ

Contest Rules — Working Draft

These are provisional terms. Registration is not open. The full official rules must be finalized and legally reviewed before accepting entry fees.

Eligibility and registration

Entrants must be at least 18 years old. International participation is planned, subject to applicable laws and payment availability. One entry is permitted per entrant per round. The proposed fee is $20 USD, processed through Zeffy. Registration closes when prompts are released.

Prompts and submission

Each round includes one mandatory cliché and seven curiosities, at least one of which must be meaningfully incorporated. The word limit is 1,000 words. Stories must be original, previously unpublished fiction created specifically for the contest. The official contest schedule and deadline will be announced in Eastern Time.

Originality and AI

Entrants must write their own work. Generative AI may not be used to brainstorm, draft, edit, or revise entries. Ordinary spell-check and voice-to-text assistance are permitted. Plagiarism, unauthorized use of protected fictional worlds or characters, and other violations may result in disqualification.

Content and conduct

Submissions must comply with applicable law. The contest does not accept erotica or work promoting hatred or discrimination. Harassment of judges, organizers, or other entrants may result in disqualification and exclusion from future rounds.

Judging and feedback

Judging is blind. Judges consider structure, prose, character, originality, and prompt integration. Every eligible submission receives feedback. Stories missing required prompts may receive feedback but will not be eligible for prizes. Disqualified entries are not guaranteed feedback or refunds. Decisions are final, subject to the finalized official rules.

Prizes and publication

The proposed awards are one $150 USD grand prize and three $50 USD runners-up. Writers retain copyright in their stories. Publication and promotional permissions will be governed by a limited, separately clarified license in the official terms. Payment methods and any applicable restrictions will be announced.

Cancellation and changes

If Ubergroup cancels a round or cannot complete it as promised, registered entrants will receive refunds. Material changes to a round’s terms should be communicated directly to participants. An official governing-law provision and eligibility exclusions will be finalized before registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to use all seven curiosities?

No. You must use at least one.

Do I get feedback if I don’t win?

Yes, provided your submission is eligible under the official rules.

Can I enter from outside the United States?

International entry is planned, but final country eligibility and prize-payment methods have not yet been confirmed.

Who owns my story?

You retain copyright. Any permission for the contest to publish a winning story will be specified in the final terms.
